TTL callback research

TTL control locked

This release isolates only whether the proven four-byte return-zero callback survives at the lower R4.0 entry address.

This page sends no TTL or diagnostic request. A separately authorized post-install qualifier may send the same exact command=ttl,arg=x request used for R4.0; this firmware ignores its fields, stores nothing and always returns zero.

What this increment proves
Earlier
R3.8 proved the same four-byte leaf at 0x06001521 with a ttl/off request. R4.0 moved to 0x06001341, added a 124-byte parser component, low-page field-name data and four helper calls, then reset on ttl/x.
R4.1
The callback remains at R4.0's lower entry 0x06001341, but its entire body is reduced to the proven four-byte return-zero leaf. The former parser tail and field-name region remain zero.
Boundary
A complete response with stable identity proves only that the lower entry and unchanged ttl/x request shape survive without parser work. A reset points to the lower entry or request-shape delta. TTL remains unavailable either way.

The existing router UI, Messages, Engineering mode, hidden debug profile and stock packet forwarding remain unchanged.
