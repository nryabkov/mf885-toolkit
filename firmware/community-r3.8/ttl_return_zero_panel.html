Known
The exact command/argument-only request still produced no response and the device disappeared while R3.7 called the stock setter.
R3.8
The same diagnostic post-set slot points to movs r0,#0; bx lr. The remaining callback cave is zero and there are no calls, loads, stores, state or TTL hooks.
Decision
A response with stable identity clears callback entry/return and isolates the removed R3.7 body. Another disappearance shows the setter was not necessary, leaving generic SET dispatch or the non-null callback boundary.
